    WHO WE ARE

    Curious builders.
    Real-world problems.

    MelkoSoft is an independent, early-stage software initiative based in Türkiye, founded around an interest in embedded systems, IoT and practical artificial intelligence.

    Our initial work draws on hands-on experience with ESP32 development, wireless communication and sensor-based projects. We're taking the first steps toward turning that experience into accessible developer tools.

    ✳We're in the exploration and prototyping stage — no claims of a launched commercial product or established customer base.
